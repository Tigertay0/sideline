Your fantasy lineup over the game, timed to your stream. Works over YouTube TV, multiview included.

    Turn on "Display over other apps" for Sideline. It only draws its own box; it can't see or record what you watch.

  2. Set it up from your phone

    Scan with your phone's camera, on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. Or type the address.
